Methodology and notes

Wave register

CityWaveVendorField datesModeLanguagesSample frameBase nWeightingMOE (±pts)Source files

Vendor for Anaheim Baseline–W3 and Garden Grove W1 identified from the deck template (The Olinger Group). Vendor correspondence confirms Anaheim W4 dropped Spanish because of limited use in the prior study, and Garden Grove W2 was online plus phone within designated ZIP codes, up to n=100. MOE is BI-computed for a 50% result at 95% confidence under a simple-random-sample assumption.

Comparability rules applied

  • A KPI is trended only where wording and scale match across waves. The Anaheim "well-informed" item breaks at W4, and Garden Grove offerings items with new examples are not compared.
  • Method changes are flagged on every trend: vendor (Olinger to Common Good at Anaheim W4 and Garden Grove W2), mode (Garden Grove W2 adds phone), languages, and sample size.
  • No wave is weighted. Shifts in sample makeup carry straight into results, notably in Garden Grove.
  • Significance is labeled by source: vendor flags as published, and BI two-tailed pooled two-proportion tests at 95%. Where a wave did not report a subgroup base, the BI test is not run.
  • Bases under 50 are flagged "use caution"; bases under 30 are suppressed.
